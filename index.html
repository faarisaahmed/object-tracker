How it works

  1. Open a video. Drop it on the page or use Open video. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Box the subject. Drag a box around the person or thing to follow. Fit it snugly so it holds mostly subject and little background.
  3. Track. It follows the subject forward from the playhead. If it slips somewhere, go to that moment, box the subject again, and track from there.
  4. Pick a shape and camera, then export. Calm holds still and only pans when needed. Locked stays centred on the subject.
